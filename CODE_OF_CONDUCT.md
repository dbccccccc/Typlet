# Code of conduct

Typlet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks have no place in the project's issues, pull requests or any other of its spaces.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in the project's spaces breaks this code, tell the maintainer privately, through the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/dbccccccc). Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer decides what follows, from a private note to removing content or banning an account from the repository.
